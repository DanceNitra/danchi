Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

1. Who we are

This website and the “DANCHI Publisher” application are operated by DANCHI (Rastislav Drahoš), an independent music producer based in Nitra, Slovakia. Contact: rastislav.drahos@gmail.com.

2. What this application does

DANCHI Publisher is a personal publishing tool. It posts DANCHI’s own music content to DANCHI’s own social media accounts (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, Bluesky). It is not offered as a service to third parties.

3. What data we access

When you connect a social account, we access only:

  • your account identifier and username
  • the account type (creator / business)
  • the ability to publish content to that account
  • basic post metrics (reach, likes) for your own content

We do not access your private messages, contacts, followers’ personal data, or any other user’s data.

4. How we store data

Access tokens are stored locally on the operator’s own computer in an environment file. No social media credentials are stored on any public server. No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.

5. Legal basis

Processing is based on your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) when you authorise the application, and on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) for the operation of the publishing tool.

6. Retention

Access tokens are retained only while the connection is active. Removing the application from your social account revokes access immediately and the token is deleted.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to processing, and the right to data portability. Email rastislav.drahos@gmail.com to exercise any of these. You may also lodge a complaint with the Slovak Data Protection Authority (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR).

8. Third-party platforms

When you connect an account, that platform’s own privacy policy also applies:

  • Meta / Instagram — privacycenter.instagram.com
  • Google / YouTube — policies.google.com/privacy
  • TikTok — tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Bluesky — bsky.social privacy policy

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The “last updated” date at the top always reflects the current version.
