Data Deletion

Last updated: 14 September 2026

How to delete your data

DANCHI Publisher stores only one thing about you: an access token that lets it publish content to your own social account. You can delete it at any time.

Option 1 — Revoke access (instant, recommended)

Remove the application from your social account. This revokes the token immediately and it is deleted from our system.

Instagram / Facebook

  • Open Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites
  • Find DANCHI Publisher-IG → tap Remove

YouTube

  • Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Find DANCHI Publisher → Remove access

TikTok

  • Open TikTok → Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions
  • Find DANCHI Publisher → Remove

Bluesky

  • Open Bluesky → Settings → Privacy and security → App passwords
  • Find danchi-publisher → Revoke

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Email rastislav.drahos@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request” and the social account you want removed.

We will confirm deletion within 30 days. All tokens and any stored metrics for that account are permanently erased.

What gets deleted

  • your access token — deleted
  • your account identifier and username — deleted
  • any stored post metrics — deleted

What is NOT deleted

Content already published to your social account stays on that platform. Delete it through the platform’s own interface if you want it removed. We have no control over content once it is published.
